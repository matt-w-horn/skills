## Withdrawal strategy

We recommend the standard sequence: **spend your ISAs and cash first, then
move on to the pensions.** This keeps things simple and lets the larger
pension pots keep compounding for longer. Once you reach 59½ you have full
penalty-free flexibility across all retirement accounts, and unlike some
jurisdictions the UK applies required minimum distributions only from age
73, so there is no forced-withdrawal pressure in your sixties.

Within the pensions, remember that 25% can be taken as a tax-free lump sum.
Many retirees take this at the point of retirement to clear remaining debts
— in your case it could retire the mortgage in 2028 rather than 2029.

## Risk

scenarios we ran, your money lasted the full 35-year horizon. The failure
scenarios were concentrated in sustained high-inflation paths similar to
the 1970s. Given the strength of this result, no changes to the plan are
needed; we suggest reviewing the plan every few years and monitoring the
situation as you approach each milestone.
