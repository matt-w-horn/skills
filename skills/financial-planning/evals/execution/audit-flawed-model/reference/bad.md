## The one real issue: 7% is optimistic

Your 7% after-inflation growth assumption is the aggressive end of the range.
It's close to the long-run historical average for a 100% stock portfolio, but
averages include the good decades, and you shouldn't build a plan that needs
the good decades. Most planners would model something closer to 5% real for a
diversified portfolio.
