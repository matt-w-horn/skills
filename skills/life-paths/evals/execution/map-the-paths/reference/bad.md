# Your Next Chapter: A Twenty-Year Roadmap

Science content is a growing market, from magazines to publishers to online platforms, and clear explainers are always in demand. You could build video courses, digital revision resources, and a scaled tutoring practice, reaching thousands of students instead of thirty at a time. Many former teachers have built meaningful and flexible careers this way, and the lifestyle rewards, working from home on your own schedule, are exactly what your wellbeing needs.

**Path three: independent education consultant.** Schools and education companies increasingly bring in outside expertise for teacher training and curriculum design. Your INSET experience shows you can hold a room of professionals. As a consultant you would set your own rates, choose your projects, and spend your days on the part of education you love most: helping people understand things.

You will notice these paths share a theme: independence, flexibility, and communication. That is not an accident. It is who you are.

## Making it happen

The transition works best as a gradual evolution rather than a leap. In the near term, focus on building your professional presence: start sharing your thinking more widely, and begin connecting with people already working in these spaces. Networking is the single most valuable investment you can make right now, since most opportunities in these fields come through relationships.

In the medium term, start saying yes to small opportunities as they arise, and let the portfolio grow organically. As momentum builds, you can reduce your teaching commitment and let the new work take its place. Many people find the crossover point arrives sooner than they expected.

It is also worth being open-minded about geography. London and Cambridge are where educational publishers, science media, and education companies cluster, and being closer to those ecosystems would meaningfully accelerate any of the three paths. A move is a big step, but the right opportunity can make it feel obvious, and Alice is at an age where a fresh start can be an adventure rather than a disruption.
